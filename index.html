    Read this before quoting a number

    • Platforms count differently. Instagram and Facebook use Meta's newer views measure, X reports impressions, and YouTube reports views, now including viewing on TVs. Compare each platform only with its own previous week.
    • Reach and viewers are unique counts for a whole week, so they cannot be added across days or platforms.
    • Meta shows dates in Pacific Time, so a day's boundary sits several hours away from Indian time. X and YouTube follow their own day boundaries.
    • Instagram views include a very small number delivered through Facebook. X impressions are rounded to the nearest hundred in X Analytics.
    • Paid and organic: no ads were found in MNRE's own Meta ad account, on YouTube or in X Ads Manager, so these views are treated as organic. Ads run from other accounts, such as the agency's own ad account or a collaborating account like PM Surya Ghar, would not show up here. The agency's ad report would confirm.
    • Meta lists post times in Indian time but groups days in Pacific Time, so a post published on the morning of 16 November in India counts in the week of 9–15 November.
    • The best-content list covers Instagram only, including posts shared with MNRE by the PM Surya Ghar and Bharat RE Summit accounts. LinkedIn and posts published are not in this version.
